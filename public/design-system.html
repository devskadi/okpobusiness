01 · PRINCIPLES

Product character

OkPo should feel approachable and operational. Strong hierarchy exposes the next decision while the system handles marketplace complexity quietly.

01

Commitment first

Lead with fixed content commitments, timelines, and accountable delivery—not speculative reach.

02

Calm confidence

Use warm neutrals, restrained status colors, and generous whitespace to reduce cognitive load.

03

One clear action

Each surface should have one dominant next step. Supporting actions remain visible but quiet.
